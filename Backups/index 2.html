The Pain You're In

You've Done the Work. You've Gotten Some Help. And Yet...

You can see the pattern. You've read the books, done the therapy, made some changes. And still — some things feel completely stuck. Dug in like ticks. You can't get to the source no matter how hard you try.

Of course you feel frustrated. Of course you wonder if you'll ever fully heal. It's been a long road.

We've walked that road. We know the terrain.

Those stuck patterns aren't random. There is intelligence behind them. They are in place for a reason. We'll teach you to work with them and harvest the gifts inside.

Let's begin.

Body
You feel chronic pain, exhaustion, illness that never fully lifts. Your body has been sending the same message for years — and it hasn't been answered yet.
Mind
You feel anxious, depressed, unable to quiet your brain's looping thoughts.
Emotions
You feel grief that never resolved, rage with nowhere to go — or a numbness you can't break through no matter how hard you try.
Relationships
The same patterns. The same fights. The same distance — with a different person, or with the same one. You wonder if it will ever actually change.

"You adapted to what happened to you. The patterns that kept you safe then are causing problems now. With awareness, that changes."

Who We Are

Mark & Lynetta

We have lived the pain, the patterns, the years of trying to change — and the breakthroughs that finally did. Everything we bring to you, we have first brought to ourselves.

As a married couple, we work together across the body, the mind, the heart, and relationships — because trauma doesn't stay in one place, and neither does healing. We combine somatic work, inner child work, conscious communication, awareness practices, ancestral healing, and a soul-level framework most healing approaches never reach.

We also reach people online — through our podcast, as guests on others', and through social media — making the work accessible to anyone, anywhere.

We travel and offer workshops at retreat centers, spiritual communities, recovery centers, prisons, and Native communities. We go where the need is. We believe the people with the least access to this kind of work are often the ones who need it most.

What We Do

The work itself

Our workshops are not lectures. You will spend most of your time working directly with another person in the room — in structured pairs and small groups — doing the kind of honest, witnessed, felt work that actually moves something. By the end of two hours, a stranger will know something true about you that most people in your life don't.

Somatic Work
Your body holds what your mind has tried to forget. We find where the pattern lives in you physically — and work with it there. This moves things that talking alone never could.
Inner Child Work
The things that trigger you now were wired in early. We go back to those moments and give that younger version of you what it never got — safety, presence, and someone who didn't look away.
Conscious Communication
Most of us were never taught how to really talk to each other — or how to truly listen. You'll practice both here, with a real person, in real time. It's simpler than you think. And it changes everything.
Awareness Practices
You can't change what you can't see. We help you spot your patterns as they're happening — calmly, without shame — so they stop running you on autopilot.
Ancestral Healing
Some of what you carry isn't even yours — it came down through your family before you were born. We help you feel the difference, and let go of what was never meant to be yours to hold.
Soul-Level Framework
When you ask "why does this keep happening to me" — this is where the real answer lives. Not a theory. A felt shift in how you understand your own life.
